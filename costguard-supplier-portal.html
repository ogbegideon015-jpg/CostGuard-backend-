Why Submit to CostGuard?

One Submission. Nationwide Visibility.

BossGiddy CostGuard is the regulated middle platform between suppliers and buyers. We make your rates available to everyone who needs them — safely and professionally.

📊
Live Rate Database
Your prices feed Nigeria's first real-time construction material cost intelligence database — used by QS firms, contractors and developers daily to benchmark their BOQs.
🔗
Direct Buyer Connections
CostGuard acts as the regulated middle layer. When a buyer searches for a material you supply, your contact link and profile are presented — no commissions, no gatekeeping.
🛡️
Verified Supplier Badge
Approved submissions earn a CostGuard Verified badge, signalling credibility to buyers across Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt and beyond. Buyers trust verified suppliers first.
💰
Completely Free to List
There is no charge to submit your quotation or to be listed in the supplier directory. CostGuard's revenue comes from buyer subscriptions — not supplier fees.
🏭
Market Rate Intelligence
Once listed, you gain access to aggregated market pricing trends across your material categories — helping you understand where your pricing sits relative to the market.
📬
Seamless Procurement Flow
Every submission is delivered instantly to our Gmail-powered procurement inbox, reviewed by our team, and entered into the system — you get a confirmation email immediately.
